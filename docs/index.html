Background — Comparison with similar solutions

Before defining NEXORA's scope, three existing market solutions that address similar retail needs were evaluated, in order to identify gaps and opportunities for differentiation.

Solution Focus Advantages Disadvantages
Odoo Open-source, modular ERP with over 80 official modules (inventory, sales, CRM, e-commerce, accounting). Highly flexible and customizable; modular pricing (you only pay for what you use); large community; cloud or on-premise deployment. Steep learning curve to configure properly; can be overkill for very small businesses; often requires technical knowledge or an integrator to get full value.
SAP Business One Closed-source ERP built specifically for small and medium businesses, with strong financial and accounting capabilities. Strong financial robustness and powerful reporting; compliance certifications in multiple countries; solid support for regulated industries. Higher licensing cost; less customization flexibility compared to open-source options; slower implementation, usually dependent on certified partners.
Loyverse POS Point-of-sale application focused on small businesses (shops, cafés, mini-markets). Free basic version; very easy to use; ideal for single-location businesses with simple sales and inventory needs. Not a full ERP: doesn't cover suppliers/purchasing robustly nor advanced financial reporting; limited for businesses growing into multiple locations.

Opportunity for NEXORA

None of the three covers exactly the middle ground a Colombian retail SME needs: something more complete than a simple POS like Loyverse, but without the cost and complexity of Odoo or SAP Business One. NEXORA fits right there, integrating inventory, sales and suppliers into a single simple system, built on the Microsoft ecosystem required for this project.

The problem

Micro, small and medium retail businesses —corner stores, boutiques, mini-markets and small chains— manage inventory, sales and suppliers through disconnected systems: notebooks, spreadsheets or isolated invoicing apps.

This causes stockouts or overstock, loss of product traceability, difficulty reconciling sales against physical inventory, and a lack of consolidated information to decide on pricing or purchasing. Without a solution, the business operates on estimates instead of data.

The solution

NEXORA centralizes inventory, sales (point of sale) and supplier/purchasing management into a single system. The end-of-semester MVP is a cloud web application (Azure) with role-based authentication, minimum-stock alerts, a simple point of sale, and a basic reporting dashboard — all the business information in one place, in real time.

Justification

The project reduces the merchant's administrative workload, decreases losses from inventory mismatches, improves the shopping experience by avoiding stockouts, and lays the groundwork for a replicable product for independent retail in Colombia.

End user

Administrator / owner

Manages users, reviews sales and inventory reports, sets prices and suppliers. Needs a simple interface and visual reports.

Salesperson / cashier

Uses the point of sale to record daily transactions. Needs speed and a minimal learning curve.

Inventory manager

Records stock in and out, manages suppliers and generates purchase orders when stock drops below the minimum.

Value and return on investment

Measured through operational indicators: daily cash-close time, discrepancies between physical and recorded stock, and the reduction of stockouts thanks to automatic alerts.

  • NEXORA System

      • Stock control
        • Record stock in and out
        • Minimum-stock alerts
    • Sales Module (POS)
      • Sale registration
        • Search product by code/name
        • Apply discounts and calculate totals
      • Cash close
        • Consolidate the day's sales
        • Generate closing report
    • Suppliers / Purchasing Module
      • Supplier management
        • Register and edit supplier data
      • Purchase orders
        • Automatically generate an order when stock hits the minimum
    • Reports Module
      • Sales and inventory reports
        • Export report to Excel

Non-functional requirements

AttributeRequirementJustification
Performance Response time < 2 seconds when recording a sale. At a point of sale, every extra second of waiting creates lines and friction with the customer; 2 seconds is the threshold commonly perceived as "instant" in commercial POS systems.
Security Role-based authentication and authorization (Administrator, Salesperson, Inventory). Prevents a cashier from changing prices or viewing financial reports meant only for the owner, protecting the integrity of the business's information.
Scalability Supports catalog and history growth without redesigning the database. A growing retail business adds products and accumulates years of sales history; redesigning the database later would be costly and would interrupt operations.
Usability Simple interface in Spanish, low learning curve for sales staff. Sales staff often rotate frequently and have no technical background; a hard-to-learn system slows down daily store operations.
Portability Web application accessible from a browser on desktop and tablet. Small stores don't always invest in dedicated hardware; working on any browser-capable device lowers the adoption barrier.
Maintainability Modular, layered code, documented and tested. The project will keep growing across future sprints with new members or maintainers; the code must be understandable and extensible without rewriting it.

Triple constraint

⏱️ Time

The project runs over the academic semester (≈16 weeks), organized into sprints. Final MVP delivery is planned for the last week of the semester; Sprint 0 is already complete.

Cost

No licensing cost: free/educational tools are used (GitHub Student Pack, Azure for Students, Visual Studio Community). The real cost is the team's effort: 4 members × ~8-10 hours/week × 16 weeks ≈ 550-640 person-hours.

Scope

Includes: Inventory, Sales (POS) and Suppliers/Purchasing, with role-based authentication and basic reporting.
Excludes (future phases): advanced CRM, Human Resources, DIAN electronic invoicing, and marketplace integrations.
